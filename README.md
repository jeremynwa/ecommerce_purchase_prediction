# ecommerce_purchase_prediction
Predict whether a customer will purchase a product or not based on their demographics and activity on the e-commerce website. This was part of a university Kaggle challenge.  

Business goals:  
- Increase conversion rates by targeting likely purchasers with personalized offers.  
- Enhance marketing campaigns by delivering well-timed nudges to users who are close to making a purchase.  
- Optimize the user experience to make the purchasing journey as seamless as possible.  

Features:  
- **Age, gender, and socioeconomic status**: Data to understand demographic influences on purchasing.  
- **Number of reviews read and time of day**: Browsing behavior data capturing user interactions with the platform.  
- **Product categories, price, and discounts offered**: Product-related features affecting purchasing decisions.  
- **Number of items added to the cart**: Cart data during the session.  
- **Email interactions, engagement scores, and device usage (mobile or desktop)**: Engagement metrics to analyze user activity.  
- **Payment method and referral source**: Data to understand user payment preferences and how they navigate to ShopSmart.
